03 / What converts

What makes a site convert.

01

The five-second test

A stranger lands on your site. In five seconds they should know what you do, who it's for, and what to do next. If they have to hunt for it, they leave — and most of them never come back.

02

One job per page

A page that tries to do everything does nothing. Every page on your site gets one job — call, book, order, enquire — and every button, form and phone number serves it.

03

Proof before promises

Customers believe other customers before they believe you. Reviews, real photos, real results — placed where the decision happens, not buried on a page nobody opens.

Pretty is not the goal. Memorable and convincing is — a site people remember and trust enough to call.

06 / Questions

Before you ask.

How much does a small-business website cost?

It depends on size and scope. As a market guide, US freelancers typically charge $1,500–$7,000 for a small-business site and agencies $5,000+. We quote per project, after the free website audit, and say the number before any work starts.

WordPress or Squarespace — which should I pick?

WordPress when you need flexibility and room to grow; Squarespace when you want something beautiful, simple and easy to run yourself. We build both — the Stash Burger site is currently being rebuilt on Squarespace — and we recommend the one that fits your business, not our preference.

How long does a website take?

Most small-business sites take 2–4 weeks from kickoff to launch, depending on content and revisions. You see progress as we go, not a big reveal at the end.

Will my new website rank on Google?

A fast, well-structured site is the foundation — but rankings also need local SEO and content. That is why every project starts with the free website audit, and why most clients pair the new site with local SEO.

Do you redesign existing websites?

Yes — most of our web work is redesigns. The free audit shows what is broken or dated first, then we quote the rebuild.

Can you work with my existing logo and photos?

Yes — we build around what you already have. If your photos or logo are holding the site back, we'll say so plainly and tell you what it would take to fix them.
