Most local searches end the same way: a phone call, a booking, or a visit. The customer types “best dentist near me” or “head spa open now”, looks at the top three businesses on the map, and picks one — usually without ever visiting a website. If your business is not in that top three, you are not losing to a better business. You are losing to a better-listed one. Local SEO is the work of fixing that: the profile, the reviews, the listings and the pages that tell Google you are relevant, nearby and trusted. It is not one fix and it is not fast, but for a local business it is the highest-return marketing there is — because the customer is already looking for you.

03 / The three signals

What Google actually ranks.

01

Relevance

Does your profile say, clearly, that you do what the searcher asked for? Categories, services and website copy all answer the same question — and when they disagree, Google trusts none of them.

02

Distance

How close are you to the searcher? You cannot move your shop, but you can make sure Google knows exactly where you serve — service areas, consistent addresses, and location pages that match.

03

Prominence

How well-known is your business? Reviews, citations, mentions and links all feed this one. It is the signal you can actually grow, month after month, and it is where most of our work lands.

Most agencies sell you one of these and hope. The work is all three, every month — because Google weighs all three, every search.
